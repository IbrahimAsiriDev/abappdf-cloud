A few useful shortcuts

Drag objects onto the page. Use the corner handles to resize. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo, Ctrl+D duplicates, arrow keys move objects, and Delete removes a selected object. Hold Shift for a larger move.

The dashed area is the printable area. Save creates a new stored version. Data & page lets you edit sample JSON, headers, and footers. Designer objects are printed content; buttons and signatures are not interactive PDF fields.
